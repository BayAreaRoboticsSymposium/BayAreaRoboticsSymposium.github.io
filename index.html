About

The 2026 Bay Area Robotics Symposium aims to bring together roboticists from the Bay Area. The program will consist of a mix of faculty, student and industry presentations. For more details, see the event schedule.

We expect all attendees (whether they are affiliated with UC Berkeley or not) to adhere to the University's code of conduct. Any concerns about participant conduct may be reported directly to the organizers, or staff at the welcome desk.

Keynote Speakers

Anne Collins

UC Berkeley Psychology · Keynote #1 · 12:00-12:30

Neuro-cognitive mechanisms of flexible and efficient human learning

Read abstract and bio
Abstract

Despite major recent advances in artificial intelligence and robotics, humans remain the benchmark for efficient, zero- or few-shot adaptation to new situations. What computational principles support this unique flexibility? In this talk, I will present experimental and computational approaches to reverse-engineering the neuro-cognitive mechanisms behind human adaptability. I will highlight key cognitive principles, such as structured representations and multi-process arbitration, and open a discussion on how these biological insights might offer fresh perspectives for challenges in robot learning.

About Anne Collins

Anne Collins received an engineering degree from École Polytechnique in France and a PhD in cognitive neuroscience from Université Pierre et Marie Curie in Paris. She is now an associate professor in UC Berkeley's Department of Psychology and is also affiliated with the Helen Wills Neuroscience Institute and the Cognitive Science program. Her lab uses computational and experimental approaches to understand the mechanisms supporting flexible and generalizable learning and decision making in humans.

Andra Keay

Silicon Valley Robotics · Keynote #2 · 13:45-14:15

Humanoids: What's really happening?

Read abstract and bio
Abstract

Is our dream of having a personal robot finally in sight? The numbers are compelling, but humanoids still face challenges before we all have Rosie the Robot. A U.S.-China showdown is not the biggest challenge.

About Andra Keay

Andra Keay is Managing Director of Silicon Valley Robotics, an industry group supporting innovation and commercialization of robotics technologies. At the center of the robotics revolution, she is a global thought leader and ecosystem builder in emerging robotics sectors.
